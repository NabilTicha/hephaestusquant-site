Eighty arithmetic questions. Eight minutes. No calculator, no scratch paper. Modeled on the Optiver trader-candidate assessment.

Rules

How it works

  • Format 80 questions, multiple choice with 4 options. One question on screen at a time.
  • Time 8 minutes total — about 6 seconds per question.
  • Scoring +1 for a correct answer. -2 for a wrong answer or a skipped question. Negative scores are possible.
  • Content Multi-digit arithmetic, decimals, fractions (add/subtract with LCM), fraction-decimal conversions, percentages, squares, order of operations, signed numbers, and linear algebra. Mental arithmetic only — no calculator, no scratch paper.
  • Pass A net score of 56 is the rough Optiver pass threshold. 70+ is competitive.
